## One claim journey, from visit to payment

LUX turns slow, manual, paper-heavy insurance claims into fast, verified, paid-and-recorded healthcare transactions.

**1**

### Identify

The provider registers and identifies the patient.

**2**

### Verify

LUX checks insurance eligibility and retrieves the applicable benefits.

**3**

### Submit

The provider submits the claim with supporting documents.

**4**

### Review

AI-assisted tools flag missing information, duplicates and inconsistencies.

**5**

### Decide

The insurer approves, partially approves or declines.

**6**

### Pay

An authorized payment instruction is sent through Orange Money.

**7**

### Confirm

The provider receives payment confirmation and a digital record.

**8**

### Reconcile

LUX reconciles the transaction for the insurer and the provider.

## Three portals, one ledger

Everyone in the claim journey gets the view they need, and every transaction can be audited.

### Provider dashboard

Register patients and verify insurance, generate bills, submit claims, upload documents, track payment confirmation and view transaction history.

### Insurer portal

Verify policyholders, configure benefit rules and approval limits, review AI-flagged claims, monitor payments, reconcile and generate reports.

### Patient access

View coverage where permitted, track claims and payment status, and receive receipts and your healthcare transaction history.

### Ledger

A controlled record of claims, approvals, payments, fees and reconciliation, so every transaction can be audited.

## AI that assists. People decide.

The AI verification engine supports the process. It does not replace doctors, insurers or human decision-makers.

Claim information extractionDiagnoses, treatments, procedures, dates, medicines and billed amounts

Rule matchingCompared with the insurer’s configured benefit rules

Duplicate detectionPotentially repeated claims

Inconsistency detectionDifferences between clinical documentation and billing

Missing-information checkIncomplete claims caught before final review

Risk-based routingUnusual claims go to human reviewers

### The insurer decides

AI does not make the final insurance decision.

### A flag is not proof

An AI flag is a reason for further review, not proof of fraud.

### AI cannot move money

Claim approval and payment authorization are separate controlled processes.

One insurer and two to three healthcare providers first, with human review of every AI-flagged claim.

Interview insurers and providers, present LUX to Orange, obtain legal advice and validate the payment architecture.

**2**

Provider dashboard, insurer portal, patient access, claims workflow, AI prototype, payment sandbox and ledger.

**3**

### Pilot: months 3 to 6

Controlled pilot with one insurer and two to three providers, with limited transactions.

**4**

### Expand: months 6 to 9

More insurers and providers, including pharmacies and laboratories.
