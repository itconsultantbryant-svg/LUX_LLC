# Real Liberian photography

The public website uses documentary photographs, not AI-generated people. The previous generated scene originals have been removed and all six responsive scene sets replaced. Supplied LUX artwork and team portraits remain unchanged.

These images are labeled geographic/healthcare context, not testimonials or evidence of LUX customers, insurers, staff, facilities or Orange Money transactions. Commissioned, consented Liberian healthcare/business photography remains the preferred replacement for promotional portraits and specific workflow demonstrations.

Originals and SHA-256 provenance: `assets/photography/manifest.json`. Public machine-readable credits: `/media/scenes/credits.json`. Attribution, source and license links are visible beside each image, including the active carousel slide. Responsive images are resized, re-encoded and cropped by layout; no generative modification is applied. The CC BY-SA originals and their image derivatives remain CC BY-SA 4.0; this does not relicense unrelated application code. Rebuild with `pnpm exec tsx scripts/build-photography.ts`.

Monrovia is represented accurately without relabeling other Liberian towns as Monrovia. Ebola-response and military exercise images were reviewed and excluded from routine-care marketing. Orange Money workflow explanations use a diagram and explicit simulation notices, not fabricated photographs of actual transfers.
