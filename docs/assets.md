Public copies of supplied originals are under `apps/web/public/media/`; sources remain under `assets/`. Images have reserved dimensions and non-critical images are lazy loaded. Page heroes are decorative backgrounds with empty alt text; content illustrations keep descriptive alt text. The supplied concept artwork is AI generation, not fabricated photography of real people or transactions, and the internal provenance record is retained for rights review. The product demonstration is real HTML with only synthetic values, not AI-rendered raster text.

Hero assignment: `hero-doctor-patient` on the homepage, how-it-works, registration and accessibility pages; `family-phone` on family, contact and privacy pages; `team-at-work` on platform, about, partners, team, terms and login pages. Login, invitation and password-recovery screens use the same images in a two-panel auth layout.

The illustration pipeline is `scripts/build-illustrations.ts`, driven by `assets/illustrations/manifest.json`. It verifies each supplied source hash, applies the documented crop, and emits AVIF, WebP and JPEG derivatives plus `apps/web/public/media/illustrations/credits.json`. Source files and hashes are retained for provenance; only the derivatives are served. Seven unused supplied posters containing baked-in promotional text or unverified statistics were deliberately excluded from the repository. Final operator approval of crops and usage rights remains a publication responsibility.
